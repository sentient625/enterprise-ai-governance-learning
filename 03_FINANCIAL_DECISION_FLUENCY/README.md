# Priority 3 — Financial Decision Fluency

**Status:** Planned

This module will strengthen the financial language required to connect delivery evidence to capital decisions.

## Planned capabilities

- NPV and IRR.
- Cost of capital.
- Risk-adjusted value.
- CAPEX versus OPEX.
- Benefits realization.
- Forecast confidence and scenario analysis.
- Sunk cost versus continuing-investment validity.
- Impairment and abandonment.
- Reversibility and option value.
- Stage funding and capital release.

## Planned proof

- Plain-language financial concept guide.
- Scenario model.
- Investment continuation case.
- Decision memorandum for continue, constrain, redirect, or stop.

The objective is executive and program fluency, not representation as an accountant or valuation specialist.
