# Module 13 Participant Workbook — Impact and Control Assessment

**Before you begin:** Complete this workbook using your own reasoning before reading the [Model Answer and Review Guide](./06_Module_03_Model_Answer_and_Review_Guide.md). Submitting the model answer as your own completed work does not satisfy the completion requirement in [Module 13's lesson](../03_Module_03_Impact_and_Control_Assessment.md).

Use the fictional case in Section 7 of the lesson and your own Module 11 and Module 12 workbooks. Do not introduce confidential information from your own employer; keep any real-world parallels abstract.

## Part 1 — Impact assessment

Using the minimum specification in Section 2 of the lesson, complete an impact assessment for the benefits eligibility recommendation use case. For each harm category you identify, state the affected population, severity/likelihood, and at least one proposed mitigation.

## Part 2 — Control assessment

For the "a human reviews every recommendation" control described in the case, complete a control assessment using the minimum specification in Section 3. Produce separate findings for designed, implemented, and operating effectively, and name the specific evidence (not an assumption) supporting each finding.

## Part 3 — Assessor independence

State what level of assessor independence this use case's tier (from your Module 12 workbook) requires, per Section 4. Name who should have performed the control assessment in Part 2, and explain why the enterprise's prior reliance on the vendor's own claim did not meet that bar.

## Part 4 — Governing response

Using Section 5's four governing responses, decide what should happen next for this use case given your Part 1 and Part 2 findings. Justify your choice and state what would have to change to move to a less restrictive response.

## Part 5 — A second control

Identify one additional control the case implies should exist but that has not yet been assessed (for example, whether appeal outcomes are tracked, or whether the model has been evaluated for disparate impact). Write a control assessment plan for it: what method you would use, what evidence you would seek, and who should perform it.

## Part 6 — Linking outward

Explain, in your own words, why a completed Module 12 risk tier and a completed Module 11 inventory entry are not sufficient, on their own, to justify continued operation of a high-impact use case. Name specifically what this module adds that those two do not provide.

## Part 7 — Executive summary

In no more than 200 words, summarize your assessment findings to an executive audience: what harm categories are in scope, what the control assessment actually found (not what was claimed), and what decision you are recommending. Do not represent a claimed control as verified unless your Part 2 findings actually support that.

## Part 8 — Defense

Prepare to defend, in writing or aloud:

- why "the vendor says a human reviews it" is not the same as "a human reviews it," using your own Part 2 findings as evidence;
- one place where your own assessment still has an unresolved gap, and what would resolve it.

## Completion note

Preserve this workbook as your own artifact. A completed workbook and defense — not the existence of this template — is what the Content Build Register and Progress Record treat as demonstrated learning.
