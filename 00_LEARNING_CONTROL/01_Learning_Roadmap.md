# Learning Roadmap

## Sequence

| Priority | Target duration | Central question | Required proof |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 1. AI-agent identity, security, and governance | 30 days | How will the enterprise identify, authorize, constrain, observe, and stop acting agents? | Privileged-access lifecycle plus defended case |
| 2. Operationalize AI governance | 30 days | How does policy become an operating system of inventory, decisions, controls, evidence, and response? | Operating model and framework crosswalk |
| 3. Financial decision fluency | 30 days | How does current delivery evidence change the economic logic for continuing, constraining, redirecting, or stopping investment? | Decision model and investment-committee case |

## Priority 1 — Active

Primary capabilities:

- Human, service, workload, and agent identities.
- OAuth/OIDC and delegated authority at recognition level.
- Credential and secrets risks.
- Least privilege and least standing privilege.
- Tool and agent-to-agent authority.
- Human decision boundaries.
- Evidence and provenance.
- Monitoring, suspension, revocation, fallback, and retirement.

Required outputs:

- Completed AI Agent Privileged-Access Lifecycle.
- Completed regulated-enterprise case workbook.
- One-page decision memorandum.
- 15-minute oral defense or written equivalent.
- Interview-ready explanation.

## Priority 2 — Active (Modules 1–2 accepted; Modules 3–10 planned)

Primary capabilities:

- AI inventory and use-case classification.
- Risk tiers and approval boundaries.
- AI impact and control assessments.
- Testing, evaluation, verification, and validation.
- Model and agent monitoring.
- Vendor and supply-chain governance.
- Incident response and decommissioning.
- Evidence retention and regulatory defensibility.
- Crosswalks among AI, cyber, privacy, risk, and records controls.

Primary sources will include the current versions of NIST AI RMF, the Generative AI Profile, NIST CSF 2.0, ISO/IEC 42001 overview materials, and applicable regulatory sources.

## Priority 3 — Planned

Primary capabilities:

- NPV, IRR, and cost of capital.
- Risk-adjusted value and scenario analysis.
- CAPEX versus OPEX.
- Benefits realization and forecast confidence.
- Sunk cost versus continuing-investment validity.
- Impairment, abandonment, reversibility, and option value.
- Stage funding and capital release.

Required outcome: fluency sufficient to connect delivery evidence to executive capital decisions without claiming to be an accountant or valuation specialist.

## Integration capstone

A regulated-enterprise AI agent receives bounded authority to perform privileged administrative activity.

The capstone will connect:

- Business proposition and investment thesis.
- Risk classification.
- Identity and access architecture.
- Human authority.
- Testing and change control.
- Evidence and monitoring.
- Incident response and fallback.
- Funding and continuation gates.
