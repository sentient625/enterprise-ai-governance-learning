## Scope and learning claim

The package includes the lesson, participant tabletop workbook, model answer/rubric, source-register additions, and navigation/content-register updates. It develops executive and program-lead judgment about emergency authority, residual work, containment verification, evidence preservation, operational fallback, and governed restoration.

It does not establish learner completion, production readiness, professional authority, legal compliance, or independent peer review. A tabletop is not a live technical validation. The case, runbook structure, and restoration gates are original educational synthesis.

## Source-status review

Publisher pages checked 2026-09-18:

- NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3: final April 2025; supersedes Rev. 2; incident-response and recovery context.
- RFC 7009: Standards Track RFC / Proposed Standard, August 2013; revocation semantics and limits, including propagation and related-token behavior.
- NIST SP 800-82 Rev. 3: published final September 2023; OT safety, performance and reliability context; publisher's potential-update note is not treated as an official revision.

References and use boundaries appear in the lesson and Source Register. These sources do not prescribe this module's complete agent-response design.

## Limitations and maintenance

The simplified case supplies neither root cause nor verified downstream containment. No universal response time, recovery deadline, notification deadline, or safe shutdown procedure is prescribed. Qualified operations, safety, security, engineering, IAM, privacy, records and legal owners must adapt the concepts.

Acceptance is now unconditional: the package is merged into main and has passed independent review. A formal GitHub Release is a separate optional packaging step. Training-product and portfolio gates remain separate. Module 9 is the next content build.

Revisit when source status changes, implementation evidence changes the guidance, or reader feedback identifies an accuracy, usability, privacy, confidentiality or positioning issue.
