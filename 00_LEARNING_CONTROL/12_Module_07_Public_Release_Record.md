## Scope and learning claim

This record covers the lesson, participant workbook, model answer, source-register update, and navigation/content-register updates. ## Known limitations

The simplified scenario cannot establish the full effects of either job. Clock precision, collection times, target mappings, and source versions are intentionally incomplete. Pilot time thresholds are illustrative, not universal or regulatory requirements. Production designs require qualified architecture, security, operations, privacy, records, legal, and safety review as applicable.
