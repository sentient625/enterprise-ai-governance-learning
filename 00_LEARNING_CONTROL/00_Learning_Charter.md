# Learning Charter

## Mission

Build credible, demonstrable fluency at the intersection of AI-agent identity, cybersecurity, governance, and capital decision-making.

## Intended professional position

> Senior enterprise program leader specializing in cybersecurity, IAM/PAM, and the governed adoption of AI agents in regulated environments.

This is a direction to be earned through demonstrated work. It is not a claim that the full learning plan has already been completed.

## Learning principles

1. **Build on credible experience.** Connect new learning to actual IAM/PAM, Zero Trust, regulated delivery, governance, and program-leadership experience.
2. **Learn to lead the work.** Understand the architecture, controls, evidence, owners, decisions, and failure modes well enough to lead and challenge qualified specialists.
3. **Do not imitate an engineer.** Configuration depth is optional; false technical positioning is prohibited.
4. **Produce evidence.** Every priority ends in artifacts and an oral or written defense.
5. **Connect governance to consequence.** Controls must relate to business value, authority, operational continuity, and investment decisions.
6. **Preserve uncertainty.** Emerging standards and incomplete evidence must be labeled honestly.
7. **Use the work externally.** Mature outputs should support interviews, thought leadership, workshops, advisory conversations, and portfolio demonstrations.

## Scope

### Priority 1

AI-agent identity, delegated authority, privileged access, tool permissions, evidence, monitoring, suspension, fallback, and accountability.

### Priority 2

Operational AI governance: inventory, classification, assessment, testing, monitoring, incidents, vendors, decommissioning, evidence retention, and control crosswalks.

### Priority 3

Financial decision fluency: NPV, IRR, cost of capital, risk-adjusted value, CAPEX/OPEX, benefits realization, forecast confidence, sunk cost, impairment, abandonment, option value, and stage funding.

## Completion standard

A priority is complete only when Michael can:

- Explain its central concepts in plain language.
- Lead a structured review.
- Identify missing ownership and evidence.
- Produce the required artifacts.
- Defend a recommendation under questioning.
- State the limits of his authority and technical role.

## Repository boundary

This repository contains learning and development work. It does not replace authoritative standards, qualified professional judgment, or production control documentation.
