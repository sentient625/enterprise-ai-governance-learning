## Scope and learning claim

The package includes the lesson, participant workbook, model answer/review guide, source-register update, and navigation/content-register changes. It develops executive and program-lead judgment about specifying an impact assessment and a control assessment as two distinct artifacts, distinguishing designed/implemented/operating-effectively control findings, scaling assessor independence to a use case's risk tier, and deciding a real governing response — proceed, remediate, accept, or hold — when an assessment finds a gap.

Publication does not establish learner completion, production readiness, independent peer review, or professional authority. The assessment specifications and fictional case are original educational synthesis, not a NIST-, ISO-, or OMB-prescribed assessment form.

## Limitations and maintenance

This module does not itself specify a continuous testing pipeline, an ongoing monitoring design, or a formal TEVV methodology; those are reserved for later Priority 2 modules once drafted. The fictional case's control-assessment findings are illustrative; learners must preserve the case's genuine unresolved items (quantified override rate, vendor fairness data) as open rather than inventing a resolution. ISO/IEC 42005:2025 and NIST SP 800-53A Rev. 5 are cited for their general methodology and framing; this module does not claim conformance to either standard's detailed procedures.

Review again when source status changes (particularly further ISO/IEC 42001-family standards or NIST SP 800-53-family updates), when Priority 2's later testing and monitoring modules are drafted and this module's forward references need tightening, or when reader feedback identifies accuracy, usability, confidentiality, or positioning concerns.
