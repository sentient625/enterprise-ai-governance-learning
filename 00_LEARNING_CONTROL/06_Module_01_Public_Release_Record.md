## Known limitations

- Agent-specific identity and authorization standards continue to evolve.
- The material provides governance analysis, not a universal reference architecture.
- Organizations must adapt decisions to their sector, jurisdiction, systems, risk, and qualified authorities.
- The model answer illustrates strong reasoning; it is not the only defensible answer.
- Source status must be rechecked before material revisions or production-facing recommendations.

## Release decision

Module 1 may be shared publicly as a free educational package after this record and its linked files are merged into `main`.

Changing repository visibility from private to public is a separate owner action. Before that action, confirm that the entire repository—not only Module 1—is appropriate for public disclosure.

## Maintenance triggers

Review Module 1 when:

- A cited source changes status or is superseded.
- NIST or another authoritative body publishes materially different agent-identity guidance.
- The learning scope expands into implementation or sector-specific claims.
- A reader identifies a factual, usability, attribution, or confidentiality issue.
- A training or commercial derivative is proposed.
