- **Not started** — no evidence created.
- **In progress** — work has begun but the required proof is incomplete.
- **Ready for review** — required artifact exists and awaits challenge.
- **Demonstrated** — artifact and defense meet the completion standard.
- **Portfolio-ready** — reviewed for external positioning, confidentiality, and unsupported claims.
