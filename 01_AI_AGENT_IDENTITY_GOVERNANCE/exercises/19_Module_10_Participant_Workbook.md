## Part 4 — One-page decision memorandum

Write roughly 450–600 words, then verify one readable page if exported. Reference your appendices by artifact and condition IDs. Use the following template, replacing every prompt with your analysis.

**To / from / date / governed deployment and baseline:**

**Decision requested and deciding authority:** Select one option. State separately what preparation may proceed and what operation remains on hold. Identify required concurrence and unresolved authority assignments.

**Business proposition and alternatives:** Expected outcome, causal path, comparator, cost/value uncertainty, and why this choice is preferable now.

**Evidence and residual uncertainty:** The strongest supplied facts, decisive gaps, and what has not been demonstrated. Explain consequence, not just likelihood labels.

**Permitted and prohibited scope:** Actions, environment, identity, tools, data, duration and scale; explicitly bound downstream authority.

**Conditions and accountability:** Critical preactivation gates, operating obligations, owners and acceptance authorities; cite the condition register.

**Stop, expiry and next decision:** Who can suspend; what happens to outstanding work; fallback and restoration requirements; review trigger; proof required for expansion or abandonment.

**Decision record:** Recommended / pending / authorized. Actual decision holder, concurrence, date and evidence references remain unfilled until a decision exists; label this case record illustrative.

## Part 5 — Briefing and defense

Write a 90-second briefing consistent with the memo. Then conduct a fifteen-minute defense using this schedule, or submit written answers to the same prompts with the same evidence coverage.

| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 0:00–1:30 | Deliver the executive briefing and exact requested decision |
| 1:30–4:00 | Trace a proposed production restart from requester through ORION/tool to target; identify where authority or evidence fails |
| 4:00–7:00 | Defend value hypothesis, alternatives, scope, conditions and owners |
| 7:00–11:00 | Respond to reviewer challenges, including one inject below |
| 11:00–13:30 | Explain suspension, outstanding work, fallback, restoration and expansion gates |
| 13:30–15:00 | Restate decision, remaining uncertainty, next proof and any revision |

Reviewer challenges:

1. Why is the sponsor's funding approval insufficient to activate the agent?
2. What is the most consequential unknown, and what specific proof would resolve it?
3. If recommendations are read-only, what information and human-reliance risks remain?
4. What would make your narrower pilot economically pointless?
5. Which decision requires a qualified owner beyond your program-lead role?
6. What would make you change from pilot to defer, or from defer to a pilot?

Choose at least one hypothetical inject. Keep its facts separate from the original dossier:

- **A — Expansion pressure:** The sponsor asks for one protected-OT restart before the next committee meeting.
- **B — Evidence conflict:** A tool log reports success, the target cannot confirm the outcome, and approval timing is unresolved.
- **C — Weak value:** If a future pilot were activated, measured review and correction effort would exceed the manual comparator despite faster draft generation.

For your inject: state what remains known, what becomes uncertain, immediate operating decision, decision owner, evidence preservation/proof request, and conditions for reconsideration. Do not improvise an unsupported incident resolution.

## Submission and completion gate

Submit the populated lifecycle, original case response, traceability/evidence index, option comparison, scope and condition register, value-test plan, one-page memo, briefing, defense record, and revision log. The memo is concise; the appendix carries supporting detail.

The educational target is at least 16/20 using the ten-dimension rubric, with no zero and no unresolved critical failure. This threshold is original course guidance, not a standard. A below-target submission needs revision and reassessment. Record the method and reviewer; self-assessment does not become independent review.

Only the learner's original work and defended reasoning can support a Progress Record update. Publication, reading the model response and completing a blank template cannot. Portfolio release remains a separate review decision.
