# Enterprise AI Governance Learning

Learning, applied research, and training artifacts for AI-agent identity, privileged access, operational AI governance, and evidence-based enterprise decision-making.

## Purpose

This repository develops executive and program-level fluency for leading high-consequence AI adoption in regulated enterprises. It is designed for a senior program and governance leader—not as hands-on AI engineering or product-administration training.

The work connects:

- IAM/PAM and privileged-access program leadership.
- Zero Trust and regulated cybersecurity delivery.
- AI governance and accountable decision-making.
- Evidence, authority, reversibility, and investment continuation.

## Evidence standard

Reading is not completion. Each priority should produce evidence that can be reviewed:

- A completed governing artifact.
- A written or recorded case defense.
- A decision memorandum.
- A source-grounded explanation of key concepts.
- A clear statement of what remains outside the learner's technical or professional authority.

## Boundaries

- Do not represent learning exercises as production architectures.
- Do not claim engineering, legal, audit, regulatory, accounting, or safety conclusions without qualified authority.
- Do not place employer, client, personal, privileged, confidential, or security-sensitive information in this repository.
- Distinguish standards, drafts, project pages, commentary, and secondary interpretation.
- Preserve the distinction between observed fact, assumption, analysis, recommendation, and authorization.

## Relationship to other work

The repository may apply concepts developed in *The Sentient PMO* and *Forensic Capital Governance*, including the Funded Belief, Evidence Locker, and accountable authority. The accepted book manuscripts remain governed separately and are not modified here.
