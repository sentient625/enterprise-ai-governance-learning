# Case Studies

This area will hold completed, sanitized cases that integrate two or more learning priorities.

## Admission criteria

A case must:

- Be fictional, public, or appropriately sanitized.
- Clearly separate fact, assumption, analysis, recommendation, and authorization.
- Identify its source and professional-authority limits.
- Include the business proposition, owners, decisions, controls, evidence, and continuation logic.
- Avoid employer, client, privileged, confidential, personal, or security-sensitive information.

## Planned integrated capstone

A regulated enterprise proposes an AI agent with privileged administrative authority across corporate IT and protected operational environments.

The case will combine agent identity, operational AI governance, financial decision logic, continuity, and accountable authority.
