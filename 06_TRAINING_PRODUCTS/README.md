# Training Products

This area will contain workshop and facilitated-learning material derived from demonstrated knowledge.

Potential outputs include:

- Executive briefing.
- Facilitator guide.
- Participant workbook.
- Architecture-review tabletop.
- Agent suspension and fallback exercise.
- Decision memorandum exercise.

## Published (drafting)

- [Facilitator Guide — Modules 1–5](./01_Facilitator_Guide_Modules_1-5.md) — group-delivery sequencing, timing, and discussion prompts for the accepted Priority 1 modules. Status: Drafting; has not yet passed the gate below.

## Gate

Do not convert learning notes directly into a product. Training material must be reviewed for accuracy, attribution, source licensing, audience level, learning objectives, answer logic, and unsupported claims.
